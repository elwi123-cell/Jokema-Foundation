About the Foundation

Local commitment. Community-led progress.

The Honlly Isaac Jokema Foundation (also referred to in public reporting as HIJF) is associated with community support and social development initiatives in Morobe Province, Papua New Guinea.

Public reporting has highlighted work and engagement relating to financial literacy, education activities, support for vulnerable people, agriculture and community development. The foundation's work is presented here as a commitment to collaborating with communities and partners on practical opportunities that can support long-term wellbeing.

Our Focus

Supporting the foundations of stronger communities.

Financial Literacy

Supporting financial knowledge and informed decision-making. Public reporting has described a partnership with the Centre for Excellence in Financial Inclusion (CEFI) to bring financial literacy training to communities across Morobe Province.

↗

Agriculture & Livelihoods

Encouraging productive opportunities, agricultural engagement and pathways that can help individuals and communities strengthen their livelihoods.

◇

Youth & Social Development

Promoting constructive opportunities, positive participation and community-based approaches that support young people and social wellbeing.

▤

Education & Community Events

Recognising the role of schools and community events in learning, identity and participation. Public reporting has described support for an Erap Primary School Pre-Independence celebration in 2025.

♡

Support for Vulnerable People

News reporting has described support for vulnerable groups, including juveniles, inmates and hospital patients. Details of current assistance should be verified with the foundation.

Peace & Community Partnership

Working with local stakeholders can help communities pursue reconciliation, cooperation and longer-term development opportunities.
