Our story

Local commitment. Lasting community impact.

Jokema Foundation is presented here as a community-focused organisation with a commitment to helping people access opportunities, build practical skills and contribute to the wellbeing of their communities.

Based in Lae, Morobe Province, Papua New Guinea, the foundation’s website can be a place to share its work, recognise community partners, celebrate local achievements and explain how supporters can get involved.

Our approach is grounded in respect, collaboration and the belief that meaningful progress is strongest when communities help shape the solutions that affect them.

Our direction

Mission, vision and values

A clear purpose and shared values help guide responsible, community-led work. The statements below are proposed website copy and should be approved by the foundation before publication.

◎

Our Vision

Communities where people have the knowledge, confidence and opportunities to shape a better future for themselves and the next generation.

↗

Our Mission

To support practical community development by encouraging learning, enterprise, inclusion and partnerships that respond to local priorities.

♡

Respect & Inclusion

Listen to communities, respect local cultures and work towards meaningful participation for people from different backgrounds.

◇

Integrity & Partnership

Act responsibly, communicate clearly and build trusted relationships with communities, organisations and supporters.

What guides our work

Areas where we can make a difference

These suggested focus areas can help explain the foundation’s work. Keep only the areas that reflect its confirmed programmes and priorities.

01 / LEARNING

Education & Skills

Encouraging access to learning, practical skills and knowledge that help people participate confidently in community and economic life.

02 / LIVELIHOODS

Enterprise & Financial Literacy

Supporting awareness of money management, entrepreneurship and pathways towards sustainable livelihoods where these align with the foundation’s work.

03 / COMMUNITY

Community Wellbeing

Working with local groups and partners on initiatives shaped by community needs, shared responsibility and long-term benefit.

04 / YOUTH

Youth Opportunity

Helping young people discover their strengths, develop confidence and explore positive pathways for their future.

05 / PARTNERSHIPS

Collaboration

Connecting with community leaders, schools, civil society, public institutions and responsible partners to support shared goals.

06 / LOCAL ACTION

Culture & Reconciliation

Recognising the importance of local identity, mutual understanding and peaceful relationships in strong communities.
